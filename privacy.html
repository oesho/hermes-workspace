Privacy policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

Scope and purpose

This policy covers the owner's private Hermes Workspace OAuth integration. Google data is used to carry out tasks requested by the account owner. The integration is not offered to the public.

Google data accessed

Depending on the task and consent granted, the integration can access Gmail messages and metadata, calendars and events, Drive files including Docs and Sheets, and contacts. Granted permissions include sending and managing Gmail, managing Calendar, creating and editing documents and spreadsheets, deleting Drive files, and reading Contacts.

Processing and storage

OAuth client credentials and tokens are stored in private configuration files in the owner's Hermes installation. Hermes may retain conversation history, tool results, logs and generated files locally. Content and task context may be transmitted to the AI or tool providers selected in that installation to perform requested work. Their processing and retention depend on the owner's configuration and the providers' applicable policies. This website does not receive Google account data or credentials.

Use and disclosure

The operator does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. This integration does not use Google user data to train generalized AI models. Data is disclosed only as needed for owner-requested tasks, to operate selected services, for security, or as required by law. The integration's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Control, retention and deletion

The owner can revoke this integration through Google Account third-party connection settings, remove local OAuth credentials, and delete retained Hermes chats, logs and outputs. Revoking access stops future API access; it does not automatically remove existing local outputs or copies processed by selected providers. The owner controls retention in the local installation and must use applicable provider controls for provider-retained data.

Website and contact

These static pages contain no analytics, advertising, forms or application cookies. The hosting provider may process ordinary web-access logs. For questions, use the owner support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.
