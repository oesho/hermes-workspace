Hermes Workspace

A private, owner-operated connection between a local Hermes Agent installation and the owner's Google Workspace account. It is intended for personal use and is not a public service or a Google or Nous Research product.

What the integration does

After the account owner authorizes Google OAuth, Hermes can perform tasks involving Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Contacts. Granted permissions allow reading, sending and managing email; managing calendar events; creating, reading, editing and deleting Drive files, documents and spreadsheets; and reading contacts.

Only the owner connects an account. These public information pages provide no sign-in, token handling, application backend, or access to the owner's Google data.

Google data and task context can be processed by AI and tool providers configured by the owner when carrying out requested work. Read the privacy policy before authorizing the connection.
